# Acid Glass Instagram Series

## Format

Film the physical panel vertically at 9:16. Keep the board, a hand, and enough
of the surrounding room visible to prove this is a live embedded object, not a
screen recording. Put the first transformation inside the first second. Cut on
visual or musical impacts and make the last frame loop into the first.

Use original device footage. Meta says Instagram recommendations increasingly
favor original posts, Trial Reels can show a Reel to non-followers first, and
resharing through messaging is central to Reels discovery. Use Edits' skip-rate
feedback to revise openings rather than treating any hook as guaranteed viral.

## Episodes

### 1. This is not a video

- Hook: black panel, finger tap, instant Acid Tunnel explosion
- Overlay: `THIS IS NOT A VIDEO`
- Proof beat: quick cut to the bare board and live `45 FPS · PPA ON` HUD
- Ending: finger covers the lens into the opening black frame
- Caption: `An ESP32-P4 is drawing every frame live. Which scene should I build next?`

### 2. ASCII Acid

- Hook: `What if a terminal learned to hallucinate?`
- Shot: close macro pass over ASCII Plasma, then vertical palette swipe
- Technical beat: `256×150 procedural RGB565 → hardware 4× scale`
- Share prompt: `Send this to the terminal person in your life.`

### 3. Touch the Trip

- Hook: split-second sequence of pinch, drag, and three-finger randomize
- Shot: one continuous overhead take so the cause and effect are undeniable
- Overlay: label Macro X/Y, Zoom, Feedback only as each gesture happens
- Loop: finish on the same scene and hand position as the opening

### 4. Music Becomes Color

- Hook: audio stopped, press Play, Scope Garden wakes on the first beat
- Shot: panel and speaker in frame, then cut to eight live band meters
- Proof beat: `Same PCM drives the speaker and the visuals`
- Caption question: `Bass, vocals, or drums: what should control the next effect?`

### 5. Twelve visualizers, one chip

- Hook: twelve scenes in twelve rapid beats
- Shot: hard cut on each scene name, then hold the audience favorite for two seconds
- Overlay: scene numbers `01/12` through `12/12`
- Save prompt: `Save this as your embedded graphics mood board.`

### 6. PPA on vs off

- Hook: `The accelerator changes everything.`
- Shot: same scene with the HUD showing PPA hardware and CPU fallback
- Technical beat: explain that PPA scales/blits while the CPU creates the art
- Boundary: report measured device FPS only; never use compile estimates

### 7. Phone-controlled Acid Glass

- Hook: phone joins `AcidGlass-XXXX`, thumb hits Randomize, panel transforms
- Shot: phone and panel in the same frame
- Overlay: `No app. No cloud. The panel hosts its own controller.`
- Safety note: briefly show `OPEN CONTROL NETWORK`

### 8. Can an ESP32 run an art installation?

- Hook: panel alone in a dark room, then reveal it embedded among reflective surfaces
- Shot: wide environmental view, close board detail, remote control from across the room
- Story: offline generative object, touch interaction, optional local C6 control
- CTA: `Where would you install one?`

### 9. Build the wildest preset

- Hook: show the final look first
- Shot: speed, warp, feedback, symmetry, palette, then save to preset slot
- Participation: publish exact values in the caption so viewers can recreate it
- Comment prompt: invite six parameter values for the next community preset

### 10. The full Acid Glass performance

- Hook: strongest transformation from the previous nine posts
- Shot: 20–30 second continuous music performance with no explanatory pause
- Overlay: only scene title, real FPS, and `ESP32-P4`
- Ending: music stop collapses into the episode-one black frame

## Review loop

Publish early episodes as Trial Reels where available. After each post, record:

- skip rate at the opening
- average watch time and completion
- rewatches
- shares and saves
- profile visits
- which scene/palette comments request

Change one variable per follow-up: opening frame, hook copy, length, camera angle,
or scene. Keep the physical proof shot and original footage constant.

## Safety

Use the default safe-flash setting in public footage. Avoid rapid full-screen
luminance reversal and add a photosensitivity warning before publishing any
deliberately strobing private performance preset. Public language should use
`psychedelic`, `acid`, `glitch`, `ASCII`, and `generative art`, not promote drug
use or imply that the device produces a medical or psychoactive effect.
