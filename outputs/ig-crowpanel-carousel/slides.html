The real problem

The app was ready. The Wi-Fi coprocessor was not.

BeforeThe P4 app compiled, but the hosted C6 link hung during the Wi-Fi handshake.
AfterThe C6 firmware was recovered, Wi-Fi came up, and live aircraft/API fetches started working.

Hardware work needs proof states: compile-ready, uploaded, and actually field-proven are not the same thing.

The lesson

Make the device explain its own state.

The best hardware dashboards do not just look good. They show live data, stale data, fetch errors, and enough context to debug without guessing.
